The training split

Three training days, in order. If you miss a day, skip it and move to the next one. After legs, start again with chest and back.

01Chest & back02Arms & shoulders03Legs & abs
Day 01

Chest & back

Lat pulldown

2 sets · 6–10 reps

Use the medium MAG grip. Keep your torso steady and pull your elbows toward your hips.

Machine chest fly

3 sets · 6–10 reps

Rest your shoulder blades against the pad. Keep a soft bend in your elbows and picture bringing them together.

Chest-supported T-bar row

2 sets · 6–10 reps

Keep your sternum against the pad. Let your elbows move slightly out as you row toward your ribs.

StairMaster

At least 30 min

Finish the session on the stairs.

Day 02

Arms & shoulders

Machine shoulder press

2 sets · 6–10 reps

Press up smoothly. Lower with control to around a 90-degree bend at the elbows.

Lateral raise

2 sets · 6 reps

Use the lateral raise machine if available, or use dumbbells. Choose a weight you can lift without swinging.

Machine preacher curl

2 sets · 6–10 reps

Keep your upper arms against the pad and shoulders still. Lower through a comfortable range and curl back up.

Assisted dips or V-bar pushdowns

2 sets · 6–10 reps

Choose assisted dips first. Lower only as far as you can control. For cable pushdowns, keep your elbows close to your sides.

Incline treadmill walk

30 min

Set the incline to 13% and speed to 3.5 mph, assuming the treadmill uses mph. Adjust to a pace you can maintain without leaning on the rails.

Day 03

Legs & abs

Romanian deadlift or back extension

2 sets · 6–10 reps

For RDLs, soften your knees, push your hips back and keep the weight close. Stand tall by driving your hips forward. Your other option is a 90-degree back extension.

Hack squat or goblet squat

2 sets · 6–10 reps

Choose your preferred squat. Use a stance that lets you keep your whole foot planted as you lower.

See goblet squat

Leg extension

2 sets · 6–10 reps

Extend your knees, then lower the pad with control.

Leg curl

2 sets · 6–10 reps

Bend your knees to draw the pad back, then return slowly.

Hip abductor machine

2 sets · 6–10 reps

Press your knees apart, then bring them back together with control.

Hip adductor machine

6–10 reps per set

Bring your knees together, then slowly let them open. Choose your set count.

Crunches

Your choice of reps

Choose your favorite crunch variation. Floor, cable or machine crunches all fit.

Before the gym

3 hours before · Have your bigger meal.

1 hour before · Choose a light, carb-rich snack, such as a banana or toast.
